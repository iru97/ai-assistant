## 3. How We Store and Protect Your Information

### 3.1 Data Storage

**Where Your Data Lives:**
- Cloud Storage: Supabase (hosted on AWS in the United States)
- Infrastructure: Amazon Web Services (AWS) data centers
- Local Storage: On your device (for offline access)

**Encryption & Security:**
- ✅ **Encryption in transit:** All data sent between your device and our servers uses industry-standard TLS/SSL encryption (HTTPS)
- ✅ **Encryption at rest:** Your data is encrypted when stored on Supabase servers
- ✅ **Password security:** Passwords are hashed using bcrypt (one-way encryption, we cannot see your password)
- ✅ **Secure authentication:** We use Supabase Auth with industry best practices
- ✅ **Regular security audits:** We review our security practices regularly
- ✅ **Limited access:** Only essential technical operations have access to infrastructure (no one reads your journals)

### 3.2 Data Retention

**Active Accounts:**
- We store your data indefinitely while your account is active
- You can delete individual entries or all data anytime in Settings

**Deleted Accounts:**
- **30-day grace period:** When you delete your account, we keep your data for 30 days in case you change your mind
- **Permanent deletion:** After 30 days, your data is permanently deleted from our servers
- **Backup retention:** Backups are retained for 90 days for disaster recovery, then permanently deleted

**You can request immediate deletion by contacting privacy@journalsafe.com**

### 3.3 Data Backups

We maintain encrypted backups of our database for disaster recovery purposes only. Backups are:
- Encrypted at rest
- Stored on AWS
- Retained for 90 days
- Never used for any purpose other than disaster recovery
- Deleted permanently after 90 days

## 5. Your Privacy Rights

You have significant rights regarding your personal information. Here's what you can do and how to do it:

### 5.1 Access Your Data
**Right:** See what personal information we have about you
**How:** Contact privacy@journalsafe.com with subject "Data Access Request"

### 5.2 Export Your Data (Data Portability)
**Right:** Download all your data in a machine-readable format
**How:** Settings → Data Management → Export Journal Entries → Choose JSON format

**What you get:**
- All journal entries (text, dates, moods)
- All photos (as downloadable files)
- Settings and preferences
- Account information

### 5.3 Correct Your Data
**Right:** Update or correct inaccurate information
**How:**
- Account info: Settings → Profile
- Journal entries: Edit any entry directly in the app
- Or contact privacy@journalsafe.com

### 5.4 Delete Your Data

**Delete Specific Entries:**
- Tap any journal entry → Delete → Confirm

**Delete All Data (Keep Account):**
- Settings → Data Management → Delete All Data → Confirm

**Delete Account (Everything):**
- Settings → Account → Delete Account → Confirm
- This deletes your account AND all data permanently after 30 days

### 5.5 Opt Out of Communications
**Right:** Stop receiving notifications or emails
**How:**
- Notifications: Settings → Notifications → Toggle off specific types
- Emails: Click "Unsubscribe" in any email, or disable in Settings

### 5.6 Restrict or Object to Processing
**Right:** Limit how we use your data
**How:** Contact privacy@journalsafe.com

### 5.7 Withdraw Consent
**Right:** Revoke permission for data processing
**How:** Disable sync in Settings or delete your account
